Account and Data Deletion Policy

Last Updated: May 24, 2025

Unic Otantic Akış (hereinafter referred to as "we", "us", or "our") places great importance on the privacy and data control of our users. This page explains how you can delete your account and associated data, and how this data is processed.

1. Your Right to Account Deletion

You have the right to delete your account created while using our application and all personal data associated with this account at any time. This process complies with Google Play Store policies and applicable data protection laws.

2. What Data is Deleted?

When you delete your account, all personally identifiable information (PII) and user-generated content that we associate with you will be permanently deleted from our systems, including but not limited to:

  • Your credentials stored via Firebase Authentication (email address, username, encrypted password, etc.).
  • Your profile information (if any, such as your name, profile picture, etc.).
  • All content you have created within the application (e.g., your saved games, scores, settings, etc.).
  • All other data related to your application usage that can be directly associated with you.

Important Note: The account deletion process is irreversible. It will not be possible to recover your data after the deletion process is complete.

3. Statement on the Use, Processing, Copying, and Sale of Your Data

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data:

  • Data Usage: The data we collect is used solely to provide you with a better service, ensure the functionality of our application, and improve the user experience.
  • Data Processing: Your data is processed in accordance with the purposes stated in our Privacy Policy and in compliance with the law.
  • Data Copying and Storage: Your data is stored on secure servers (e.g., the Firebase platform) and protected against unauthorized access. Unnecessary copies of your data are not created.
  • Data Sale: We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes under any circumstances. Your data may only be shared in the limited circumstances specified in our Privacy Policy (e.g., legal obligations or mandatory sharing with service providers for technical infrastructure) and with your knowledge and consent.
  • Data Retention Period: When you delete your account, your associated data is permanently deleted from our systems within a reasonable time. Some data may need to be retained for longer periods due to legal obligations or legitimate business purposes (e.g., anonymized analytical data), but such data can no longer be personally associated with you.

4. How to Delete Your Account From Within the Application

Please follow the steps below to delete your account and associated data. These steps are illustrated below with relevant screenshots from within the application:

Step 1: Click on Your Profile Picture

Open the application and click on your Profile picture at the top. Access the "Delete My Account" button located in the My Profile section.

Example: Settings or Profile screen.

Step 2: Account Deletion Option

In the Settings menu, click on the "Delete My Account" button.

Example: Screen where the "Delete My Account" button is located.

Step 3: Confirm the Deletion

When you click on the account deletion option, you will see a confirmation message warning you that the process is irreversible and all your data will be permanently deleted. If you are sure you want to proceed, please confirm the deletion (e.g., by clicking "Yes, Delete My Account" or "Confirm").

If you logged in a long time ago, you will be asked to log in again for your security and to confirm that you are performing this action. If you have recently logged in, your account will be immediately deleted with all data being permanent. If you log back into the application after your account is deleted, you can start fresh with a new UID number. Your account will not be banned.

Example: Warning message asking for account deletion confirmation.

Once you confirm, your account deletion process will be initiated, and your data will be permanently deleted from our systems. You will be automatically logged out of the application when this process is complete.
