Unic Otantic Akış

Unic Otantic Akış ("we", "us", "our") takes the safety of all users who use our platform, especially children, extremely seriously. This Child Safety Policy explains our stance against child sexual abuse and exploitation (hereinafter referred to as "CSAE") and the measures we take to combat such content and behavior.

This policy has been prepared to comply with the Google Play Child Safety Standards Policy and other relevant legal regulations.

1. Our Commitment Against CSAE

Unic Otantic Akış strictly prohibits the creation, sharing, distribution, or promotion of CSAE materials (including Child Sexual Abuse Material - CSAM) on our platform. We have a zero-tolerance policy towards any content or behavior that sexually exploits, endangers, or harms children. The accounts of users involved in such activities may be immediately suspended or terminated, and relevant legal authorities may be notified.

Key issues covered by CSAE include (but are not limited to):

  • Images, videos, or texts depicting or encouraging child sexual abuse.
  • Attempts to communicate with children for sexual purposes (grooming).
  • Sexual blackmail or coercion targeting children.
  • Child trafficking for sexual purposes.
  • Any other material and behavior involving the sexual exploitation of children.

3. Handling CSAM and CSAE Materials

When CSAM or other CSAE materials are detected on our platform or reported to us, we take the following steps:

  • We immediately remove the relevant content.
  • We may suspend or permanently terminate the account of the user who created or shared the content.
  • We report the situation to authorized legal authorities such as the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or local law enforcement, in accordance with our legal obligations.
  • We proactively work to prevent the spread of CSAM on our platform using technological tools (e.g., hash matching technologies). [Specify if you use such technologies.]
